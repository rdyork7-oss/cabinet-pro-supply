## What “wholesale online” should actually mean

Many online dealers compete on a short list of imported stock SKUs and a plywood-box headline. That can work for a simple box run. For a full kitchen, you want door and finish depth, reliable lead times, and someone who will catch missing fillers and appliance panels before the truck leaves.

We work with name-brand and North American manufacturers we represent—lines that have been around for decades and deliver nationwide. Once the order is placed, cabinets arrive on schedule and in good shape. Most of our follow-up is adding a little molding or a matching cabinet for another room—not chasing damaged freight or missing parts.

## Stock, semi-custom, and custom at wholesale

Wholesale pricing is what catches people’s interest—and it should. Quality and service ought to be a given from any company you work with. Even at the lowest possible price, some projects do not justify the cost of full custom cabinetry. Fortunately, many manufacturers offer scaled-back versions of their custom products as semi-custom and stock lines. Those cabinets use the same high-quality materials and finishing processes, with fewer options available—and they are often the best value on the market today.

CabFabs fills the stock/value lane when speed and a simple painted shaker package fit the job. We do not dress stock up as made-to-order American custom. When the kitchen needs more door styles, finishes, or modifications, we move you into the name-brand programs above.

## How to buy the right way

A few habits separate a smooth wholesale order from a headache:

- Start with the whole kitchen—layout, appliances, molding, and fillers—not just a door style and a price list.
- Ask who designs the takeoff and who stands behind the warranty.
- Compare construction and finish programs across brands, not just the plywood-box checkbox.
- Match the product tier to the job: stock for speed, semi-custom for most remodels, custom when inset, specialty finishes, or deep mods matter.
